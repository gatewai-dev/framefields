`framefields/dna`: read DNA character files (the rig format MetaHuman characters export to; file versions 2.1 to 2.8) and write them as GLB files: joints at their neutral pose with rest orientations, the meshes of a LOD with skin weights and blend shapes as named morph targets, axes and units converted to glTF. Based on OpenRigLogic (MIT, Epic Games). Forge skeletons take rest orientations (`orient`).
