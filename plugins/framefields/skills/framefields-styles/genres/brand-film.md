## Beats

1. **Open**: an image or a word that sets the mood, with no explanation.
2. **Build**: images and short lines that add up, faster as the music builds.
3. **Peak**: the strongest image or line, on the music's peak.
4. **Resolve**: slower; the meaning lands.
5. **Name**: the brand name alone, then black.

## Audio

Music carries it: choose (or generate) the track first and cut to it. Sound design is sparse and deliberate. A voice, if any, is a few lines, not a narration.

## On screen

Few words (a line every few seconds, never a paragraph). The name appears once, at the end. No features, no prices.
