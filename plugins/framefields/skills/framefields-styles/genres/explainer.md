## Beats

1. **Question**: the one thing this answers, in the first 5 seconds, as a line on screen and in the voice.
2. **Context**: why it matters, in one or two images.
3. **Steps**: three to five, each one visual idea, numbered on screen.
4. **Payoff**: the answer, shown, not just said.
5. **Recap**: one line, then the end card.

## Audio

The voiceover leads; write the script first and time every visual to it (generate voice, then transcribe it with word timings, then place cues on words). Music sits 18–24 dB under the voice, ducked further under dense lines.

## On screen

Every spoken idea has a visual; key terms appear as on-screen labels when they're said. Captions when it will play muted. Nothing on screen the voice doesn't explain.

## Build it with

`components/captions` from the transcript; `components/callout`, `components/annotate` and `components/counter` for the steps.
