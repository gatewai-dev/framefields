## Beats

1. **Tease**: a detail, a silhouette or the problem, never the whole product.
2. **Reveal**: the product in full, on the music's first big hit.
3. **Features**: three, no more; each one a shot and three to five words.
4. **Name**: the product name alone, held.
5. **Call to action**: when, where, or the price, then out.

## Audio

Music-led: a track that builds to the reveal and drops out for the name. Sound design on every move (whooshes on reveals, a soft hit on each feature). A voice only if the user asks; then very few words.

## On screen

The product in every shot after the reveal. Feature lines short and concrete ("All-day battery", not "Unleash your potential"). The name and call to action are the last two things on screen.

## Build it with

`components/device-frame` and `components/app-frame` for software; `components/logo-reveal` for the name; `AudioSignal` for hits.
