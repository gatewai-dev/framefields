# Gemini 4 Neon — 20-Second WebGPU Showcase

A 20.0-second (600 frames, 1920×1080, 30 fps) code-first launch film demonstrating Gemini 4 Neon capabilities rendered natively on WebGPU (Dawn) with Framefields.

## Structure (120 BPM · 10 Bars)

1. **Act I — The Neon Singularity (0–4s):** Custom WGSL liquid plasma caustic field, beveled iridescent obsidian-chrome 4-point Gemini Star prism (`Layer3D.extrude`), orbital rings, GPU sparks, and kinetic Slug typography.
2. **Act II — Deep Reasoning & 10M Context (4–8s):** 1,800-shard morphing 3D neural constellation (`Layer3D.instances`), glowing `Layer3D.tube` synaptic highways, live 10M token counter, and native `Layer.chart`.
3. **Act III — Agentic Execution (8–12s):** Floating 3D glass IDE streaming code over a glowing fiber beam into a live sculpted `Layer3D.sdf` gyroid-sphere manifold.
4. **Act IV — Omni-Modal & Physical World (12–16s):** Deterministic `Physics.world3d` + Voronoi `world.fracture` shattering a crystalline monolith into 48 cyan-core shards, GPU `Layer3D.swarm` sparks, and a live audio-reactive spectrum dock.
5. **Act V — Monumental Hero Lockup (16–20s):** 1,500 luminous shards converging into the faceted 4-point Gemini Star with sweeping metallic glint and held hero typography.
