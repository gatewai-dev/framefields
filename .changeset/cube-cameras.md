Cube cameras: `lens.projection` `"equirectangular"` (360° panoramas) and `"fisheye"` (up to 360°), drawn as six mipmapped cube faces and remapped. `relativity: { speed, direction, doppler, beaming, exposure }` films a scene as seen near the speed of light: aberration, spectral Doppler shift and beaming, animatable with `relativitySpeed`. Lens options are now typed as authored, so fields with defaults may be left out.
