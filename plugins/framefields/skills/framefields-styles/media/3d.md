## Pipeline

1. Block the scene with primitives first (`Layer3D`), camera and one light; check the framing on a frame.
2. Then materials, then `Layer.environment` for image-based light, then the camera animation.
3. A user's own model (`.glb`) replaces the primitive once the framing works.
4. Type stays 2D on top unless the look asks for 3D type.

## Cost

Free, but renders slower than 2D: draft at a low resolution, and keep shadows and depth of field for the final pass.

## Verify

Frames at the start, middle and end of each camera move; check that nothing clips the near plane and that the product's silhouette reads against the ground.

## Build it with

`Layer3D`, `Layer.model`, `Layer.environment`, `Layer.camera`, `CameraAnimation`.
