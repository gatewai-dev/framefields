A hand-drawn arrow's head no longer spins. `Annotate`'s arrow aimed its head from the tip to the last point of the wobbled shaft, a segment a few pixels long that the boil moved on every re-pose, so the head swung round (fully, on short arrows), and the wobbled shaft stopped short of it. The head now follows the clean curve's direction a head's length back from the tip, the same in every pose, and the hand lets go of the shaft toward the tip so the two always meet.
