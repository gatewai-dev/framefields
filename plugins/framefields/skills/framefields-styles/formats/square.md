## Frame

1080×1080 at 30 fps. Centered compositions; one subject, one line of type. The frame reads at thumbnail size, so shapes and type are bold.

## Timing

Most views are muted and scrolled past: the first frame works as a still, and the message reads without sound (on-screen text for every spoken line). Cuts every 2–4 seconds. 6–30 seconds is the sweet spot.

## Safe areas

60 px in from every edge; feeds crop corners into rounded tiles.
